**Six new genome assemblies from the Magic population**
The Peanutbase DataStore has now the following six genome assemblies shared by Ethan Thompson from the  Institute: <br/>
<a href="https://data.legumeinfo.org/Arachis/hypogaea/genomes/13M.gnm1.1GWQ/">13M(Georgia-13M)</a>, 
<a href="https://data.legumeinfo.org/Arachis/hypogaea/genomes/97R.gnm1.N3PF/">97R(SunOleic 97R)</a>, 
<a href="https://data.legumeinfo.org/Arachis/hypogaea/genomes/C20.gnm1.RQ8X/">C20(GT-C20)</a>, 
<a href="https://data.legumeinfo.org/Arachis/hypogaea/genomes/NC.gnm1.K6PZ/">NC(NC94022)</a>, 
<a href="https://data.legumeinfo.org/Arachis/hypogaea/genomes/TNV.gnm1.N7LF/">TNV(TifNV-High O/L)</a> and 
<a href="https://data.legumeinfo.org/Arachis/hypogaea/genomes/WS16.gnm1.3F94/">WS16(GP-NC WS16)</a>.
