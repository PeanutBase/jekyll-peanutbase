**Comparative gene expression tool updated to latest gene model versions**<br/>
The <a href="https://conekt.legumeinfo.org/" target="_blank">CoNekT@LIS</a> comparative gene expression tool has now an updated build with the most recent annotation versions of six crop legumes. This version includes Tifrunner.gnm2.ann2 gene models. The tool also shows coexpressed genes and their immediate network.
