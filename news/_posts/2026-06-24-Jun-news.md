**<i>Arachis cardenasii</i> genome has now been annotated**<br/>
The annotation data files are now in our <a href="https://data.legumeinfo.org/Arachis/cardenasii/annotations/K10017.gnm1.ann1.5HQH/">DataStore</a> and it also available in our <a href="https://dev.peanutbase.org/tools/jbrowse2/?config=config.json&session=share-PAAugfVQqN&password=ehQzC">genome browser</a>. The details will be presented at the 2026 APRES conference.
