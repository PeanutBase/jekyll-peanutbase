**Find the corresponding geneID in another genome annotation version**<br/>
This search tool can find the corresponding gene ID in another related genome *via our pangene set for Arachis*. For example you can search for the BaileyII  or Tifrunner.gnm1 gene ID corresponding to the most current Tifrunner gnm2.ann2 gene ID versions. Go to the <a href="/tools/translate/">Gene Model Translation / Correspondence</a> tool.<br/>
(It is also available from the Home page Tools panel as **Gene-name translator**)<br/>
More about the tool and details of pangene are also available on this page (click the 'Background and Methods' subheading).
